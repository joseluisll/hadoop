## Emitter-unblocking curation (this commit)

1. **`HSQL License` section** — 3.5's manual manifest lists
   `org.hsqldb:hsqldb:2.7.1` under an HSQL License section whose text
   the trunk store never carried, but the manual tarball ships
   `licenses-binary/LICENSE-hsql.txt`. Ported verbatim into
   `dev-support/licenses/texts/LICENSE-hsql.txt` with a component alias
   row `hsql → hsqldb` (staleness-checked: hsqldb is listed).
2. **`baseline-browser-mapping 2.10.0` section — a manual-artifact
   ghost, removed from the committed LICENSE-binary.** The package
   ships nowhere in the official 3.5.0 tarball (full enumeration:
   0 matching files), and the section body is three lines of prose
   listing nothing. The section is inherited from trunk's UI
   dependency set; the manual manifest never pruned it. Evidence
   preserved in the comparison package
   (`analysis-3.5/sources/tarball/`, enumerated `archive-jars.tsv`).
   NOTE: the committed LICENSE-binary on this branch is transitioning
   to the system's artifact (the R40 flow replaces it from the run's
   archives); the pristine manual baselines — release-shipped and
   branch-committed — are preserved unmodified in the comparison
   package sources and remain the baseline under assessment.

After this curation the emitter runs clean against the branch manifest
(71 texts emitted, verified locally; the exact CI command).
